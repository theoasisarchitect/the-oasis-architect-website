Our Story

Meaning does not happen by accident.

To curate a home, an event, or an experience for someone else is to shape memory in real time. It is deciding what will be felt, what will be remembered, and what will stay long after the moment ends. Every detail matters. Not because it looks good, but because it creates an emotional structure that people move through.

To curate is to care.

To organize is to honor.

To make space is to make meaning possible.

It started in kindergarten. Even then, it was something innate within me.

Over the years, I became the unofficial director of operations for my friends and family hosting birthdays, planning trips, organizing homes as people stepped into new chapters. I was always the one they turned to.

Professionally, I built a career in operations and strategy, working across finance and systems implementation. I have always been drawn to bringing structure to complexity and creating systems that allow things to flow with ease.

Now, I am stepping fully into what I do best and what I genuinely love.

May 1995 · Kindergarten Report

Need to Know

Frequently asked
questions.

Where do you serve clients? +
We are based in Arlington, Virginia and serve clients throughout the DMV Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia as well as New York. Virtual sessions are available for clients outside these areas.
What can I expect? +
Every project begins with a complimentary 15 20 minute intro call virtual and easy. From there, we move into your session with a full walkthrough, thoughtful questions, and a plan built around your lifestyle. At the end, you receive a summary and next steps. Nothing is cookie-cutter. Everything is designed for you.
How does the organizing process work? +
We follow a 7-step process: Walkthrough → Sort + Edit → Design + Strategize → Source → Implement → Grand Reveal → Final Steps. You are involved at every decision point, and nothing leaves your home without your say. The goal is a space that feels calm, functional, and easy to maintain long after we are done.
What are the payment terms? +
A $100 deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance is due the day of your session or prior to your event. Consultations are paid in full at booking.
How can I pay? +
We accept Zelle, Venmo, and Stripe (credit and debit cards). Payment details are sent at booking confirmation.
What is the cancellation policy? +
We ask for at least 48 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made less than 48 hours in advance are non-refundable. Exceptions are considered for emergencies.
Can I bundle services? +
Yes and we love it. Services are designed to complement each other naturally. Organize your space and photograph the result. Plan an experience and let us handle every detail. Align your strategy and then build the systems to support it. Mention your interest during your intro call and we will create a custom proposal.
